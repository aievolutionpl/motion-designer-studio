# Motion Style Library

## Flat Vector Pop
Clean flat 2D, bold contours, solid fills, limited palette. Explain­ers, SaaS, education. Remotion / Motion Canvas / HyperFrames.

## Ink Marker Editorial
Hand-inked marker, paper grain, irregular edges, mostly monochrome. Founder stories, editorial explainers. Remotion / AE / HyperFrames.

## Monochrome Void
Pure black/white, silhouettes, hard contrast, negative space. Premium conceptual motion. HyperFrames / Remotion / Motion Canvas.

## Polished Product Showcase
Controlled commercial product film, accurate materials/reflections, slow dolly/orbit/macro. Three.js / generative video / AE / Remotion.

## Broadcast TV Spot
Polished commercial progression, motivated cinematic light, hero shots, strong end card. Generative video / AE / Remotion.

## Organic UGC Motion
Phone-first creator framing, believable light, fast readable edit, lightweight overlays. Generative video / Remotion.

## Cel Motion
Crisp silhouettes, controlled color blocks, staged parallax, snap motion. Generative video / Remotion / AE.

## Watercolor Story
Painterly washes, paper grain, soft edges, slow reveals. Generative video / AE / Remotion.

## Technical Motion Diagram
Precise modular diagrams, semantic color, progressive construction. Motion Canvas / Remotion / HyperFrames.

## Experimental Wild Card
One deliberate convention-breaking mechanism while preserving hierarchy. HyperFrames / generative / AE / Three.js.
