# Audio Design

Treat music as part of timeline, not an afterthought.

When audio exists, determine when possible:
- BPM
- downbeat
- major energy changes/drop/breakdown
- cue points

Map important visual events to beats when it improves the edit.

Use SFX selectively: click, whoosh, hit, impact, riser, switch, success, glitch. Do not add a sound to every movement.

If no audio-generation provider/tool is available, create the cue sheet only. Never claim music/SFX were generated when they were not.
